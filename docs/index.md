Bonkverse is a fan-made platform for [bonk.io](https://bonk.io): a skin
library at [bonkverse.io](https://bonkverse.io), a replay archive at
[replays.bonkverse.io](https://replays.bonkverse.io), and the tools around
them.

## For developers

**[The Bonkverse API](api/intro.mdx)** gives your site or Discord bot bonk.io
player data from the replay archive: levels, when they were last seen, and
level history. Start with the [quickstart](api/quickstart.mdx).

## How Bonkverse is built

Platform documentation (how the services fit together, design decisions,
and what Bonkverse has learned about bonk.io) is coming here next. Until
then, each service documents itself in its repository's `docs/` folder, for
example [Bonkverse/bonkverse](https://github.com/Bonkverse/bonkverse/tree/main/docs).

Bonkverse is not affiliated with bonk.io.
